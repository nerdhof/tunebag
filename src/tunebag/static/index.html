⬡ Intents An intent is a shared plan: tag filter, optional artist filter, source toggles, and rating distribution. Assign hours (and optional expiry) per device after creating; both share one seeded sequence, and the smaller budget is the opening stretch. Switch to intent starts or continues that plan’s playback (pauses when it is already playing). Regenerate sequence rebuilds assignments; Fill now tops one intent to its full device budget on a metered link.

    ⬇ Cache Audio on this device for its assigned hours. Creating or updating an intent fills automatically; use Fill now on an Intent to top up once while metered. On a metered link, origin downloads stay at the current release plus one ahead per intent; peer copies still fill.
